When Nightlets begin stealing the healing clams that the Water Dragons depend on, the signs of an approaching war become impossible to ignore. But the danger runs deeper than anyone realizes.

Galeana, Keeper of the Water Dragons, turns to the Star Dragons for answers. What she receives is a prophecy foretelling a storm that could change the fate of the world. Only the Water Dragons and Ice Dragons can restore the balance—but their enemies are already gathering.

Together with Glacioron, Keeper of the Ice Dragons, Galeana sets out to find allies and uncover the truth behind the growing threat. With Fire and Earth rising in a dark alliance, the two dragons must discover whether the prophecy will lead their world to salvation or destruction.

Can Tide and Ice stand together before the world falls?
