What Swords Couldn't Save
Book 1 of What Remains
Fantasy

Status: In Progress

When a rebellion begins sweeping south through the kingdom, the people of Aurelith are warned to prepare for the worst. Its enemies have no intention of stopping at one kingdom. They mean to take control of both.

For Astra, this is more than a threat to her home. Ever since losing her parents, she has spent nine years secretly training in swordsmanship, preparing for the day she might face the person responsible.

When a messenger arrives with a warning, Astra sets out on a dangerous quest to stop the rebellion before it can consume her kingdom as well as neighboring Caelwyn. With the help of those who accompany her, she'll have to face an advancing enemy, uncover the truth behind the growing conflict, and fight for everything she has left.

Can one young warrior stop a rebellion before two kingdoms fall?
