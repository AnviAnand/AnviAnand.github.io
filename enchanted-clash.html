When the Black Sorcerer Malkallam returns from exile, the kingdom of Ghelder is thrown into danger. The royal family's magic is under attack, the castle is no longer safe, and an ancient enemy is preparing to claim the throne.

But Princess Anar isn't going to sit back and watch her kingdom fall.

Armed with her trusty sling, a magical pomegranate, and her unicorn and dragon companions, Anar sets out on a dangerous journey to Kerhjer Valley. With the help of friends, allies, and a mysterious prophecy, she'll have to face an army of shadowy creatures and a sorcerer determined to rule.

Can one young fairy princess break the spell and save her kingdom?
