Prologue

The lush green grass was soft under our bare feet in the beautiful spring morning. I ran after my baby brother, chasing a butterfly through the flowering trees of our garden. Clear crystal blue wings glinting in the sun, it fluttered upward, higher and higher, through the cloudless sky until it was a mere speck in the endless blue.

CLANG! I rushed Theo into the bedroom, put him on the bed, and shielded him with my own body so he could not see or be seen. I grabbed the old shield from the corner with one hand and held it up, hands shaking, to block anyone’s view and kept my eyes on the door. It cracked and a long blade pierced through. “NOT MY KIDS, YOU MONSTER!” The blade suddenly withdrew and I heard footsteps getting farther away.

Trembling, I told Theo, “Stay here. Don’t move.” Down the hallway, out the front door, through the park. “Mom! Dad! Where are you?”

I stopped dead in the middle of the park green, tears rolling down my face. “Mom. Wake up, please wake up. Dad? Come on, please.” My voice shook as I realized…. They weren’t coming back. I couldn’t raise Theo alone. He was 2. He wouldn’t understand death or loss. I couldn’t provide for one of us, let alone both.

“Come here, baby. Everything’s going to be okay. I’m here now. You’re gonna be fine. We’re gonna be just fine as long as we stick together.” I picked Theo up and rocked him gently, trying to calm his crying. A red rage came over me. Whoever did this to us, killed my parents, robbed my brother of the chance to have a father, and a real mother, not his 9 year old sister, they were going to pay.
